Research Hub

I do a lot of research as an enabler to my work as an advisor, and to inform my views on the tech, the product and the market.

Sometimes it is just for me and not shareable, either for confidentiality or for quality. Not good or useful to anybody else.

Sometimes it reaches a level of relevancy that, while nowhere near what research companies or serious analysts produce, may still be of value to a broader audience. So I decided to build this hub and share those.
