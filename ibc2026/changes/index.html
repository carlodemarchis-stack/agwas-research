IBC2026 vendor scan: what changed

Every version as it went live, newest first.

Version numbers that were bumped while building and never reached the site are not listed here, so the sequence has gaps. The notice on the scan page fires on a minor or major bump, which means a change to the data or to how the page works. Restyles and small fixes ship quietly.

v. 1.5.2 · 21 September 2026

The method is reframed around Hall 5 as the measuring stick rather than the boundary, since the scan reaches across eight halls. The confirmed-stand count corrects from eighty-one to seventy-six and the never-got-in-touch count from twenty-six to twenty-four, both following the earlier removal of 1 Legion and the folding of Stream Republic into DVEO.

v. 1.5.0 · 19 September 2026

1 Legion drops out of the scan, taking the platform tier to seven. The book and the course get a promo each rather than sharing one. The foot of the page, which had been sitting outside the reading column, is put back in it.

v. 1.4.0 · 19 September 2026

NPAW and Setplex join the scan, taking it from 127 records to 129. The platform tier goes from seven companies to eight, and the AI tallies move with it.

v. 1.3.5 · 18 September 2026

A favicon across the site: the four AI shades as bars.

v. 1.3.4 · 18 September 2026

The floating button becomes back to top rather than back to the filter bar.

v. 1.3.3 · 18 September 2026

The filter bar becomes an amber panel and the filters fill in when pressed. A floating button appears once you are down among the records.

v. 1.1.3 · 18 September 2026

The accent moves from granata red to amber, and the four-step AI shade ramp is retuned to match.

v. 1.0.4 · 18 September 2026

127 records, up from 99. The sources page arrives, and the scan starts carrying a version stamp.

First publication · 18 September 2026

99 records on a single page, published at research.aguywithascarf.com.
