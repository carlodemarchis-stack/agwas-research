IBC2026 vendor scan: sources

Everything the scan was built from, and what each source was used for.

v. 1.0.0 · updated 18 September 2026

This list is reconstructed after the fact and I would not swear it is complete. Searches that returned nothing useful are not recorded, and a handful of pages consulted early on are no longer traceable. Where a record in the scan rests on a single source, that is noted in the record itself through the depth and booth-source fields rather than here.

The primary source

201 IBC-tagged email threads received between June and 8 September 2026, read directly. This is where the scan starts and it is the one source that cannot be linked. Ninety-three of the hundred companies in scope reached me this way. Thirty-nine records were built from reading the full message; twenty-seven rest on a subject line, a newsletter snippet or a directory blurb, and are marked as such.

What was tried and failed recorded because it shaped the method

  • The IBC exhibitor directory, fetched directlyRenders through JavaScript that returns an empty template to both a plain fetch and a rendering browser. This is why the Hall 5 list had to be supplied by hand, and why no other hall was cross-referenced the same way.
  • LinkedInBlocks automated access, so posts referenced during the work could not be read.
  • A 2025 Hall 5 floor planSurfaced from an earlier conversation and used before its year was checked. It put wrong booth codes on nine records. Every Hall 5 code was subsequently re-verified against the official 2026 directory, and codes traced to the 2025 plan were removed.
